# 0015 — QR menus, restaurant pages and custom domains, without Cloudflare

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-18

## Context
Every restaurant gets a QR menu and a page where customers can order; some want their own domain. Cloudflare is blocked in Syria, so it cannot terminate TLS for custom hostnames. Vertex Shifa ADR 0015 designed this flow.

## Decision
- **Default:** every restaurant gets `<slug>.<platform domain>` (platform domain: Q1); `apps/web` resolves the tenant from the host name on every request.
- **QR codes** point to `<slug>.<platform domain>/<branch>/t/<table>` (dine-in) or `/<branch>` (menu and ordering); printed QR sheets are generated by the restaurant app.
- **Custom domain flow:** Vertex staff enter the domain in the console; the console shows a CNAME target and a TXT verification record; a job verifies DNS, issues the certificate and activates routing. States: `pending_dns`, `verifying`, `issuing`, `active`, `failed`, `expiring`.
- **TLS:** Let's Encrypt on our own servers; the reverse proxy serves only verified domains (Caddy on-demand "ask" check, or nginx per-domain configuration, following ADR 0017). Unverified domains never trigger issuance.
- **Branding:** pages show the restaurant's identity (ADR 0010) and always "Powered by Vertex Food".
- **Security:** rate limits and a proof-of-work challenge (ALTCHA) on OTP and order forms, nginx limits, fail2ban; no third-party CDN.
- **Speed:** server-rendered pages, compressed images in several sizes, no third-party scripts or fonts.

## Consequences
- Custom domains are a catalog feature (ADR 0013).
- Apex domains need CNAME flattening or an A record; the console explains both.
